Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

You can cancel MY FLEET at any time. Fees for a month already started are not refunded, except in the cases listed below.

Cancellation

  • To cancel, contact us at parmargeeta985@gmail.com or +91 82006 50810, or use the cancel option in the app if available.
  • Your service stays active until the end of the current billing period. You will not be billed after that.
  • To stop billing for a vehicle you have sold, remove it from your account before your next billing date.

Refunds

We refund the payment in full if:

  • you were charged twice for the same bill;
  • you were charged an amount higher than your bill; or
  • you paid but the service was not activated because of a fault on our side.

To request a refund, write to parmargeeta985@gmail.com within 7 days of the payment with your registered mobile number and payment details.

Refund timeline

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 working days to the original payment method (UPI, card or bank account). Your bank may take a few more days to show the credit.
