Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

MY FLEET ("we", "us") is a fleet management software service operated by Geetaben Rahulbhai Parmar, sole proprietor, Vadodara, Gujarat, India. This policy explains what personal data we collect through the MY FLEET app and website https://erp.aakashfinance.com, and how we use and protect it.

Information we collect

  • Account details: name, mobile number, email address and business name.
  • Vehicle details: registration numbers and the vehicle, document and due-date information you add or that we fetch for your vehicles.
  • Payment records: invoice amount, date and payment status. Payments are processed by Razorpay; we do not collect or store your card, UPI or bank login details.
  • Technical data: device type, app version and basic usage logs, used to keep the service working.

How we use your information

  • To provide the service: manage your vehicles, show due dates and send reminders.
  • To create your monthly bill and send payment links, receipts and invoices.
  • To send service messages by SMS, WhatsApp or email, including login OTPs.
  • To provide support, prevent misuse and meet legal requirements.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share data only with service providers that help us run MY FLEET (hosting, SMS/OTP and email providers, and Razorpay for payments), and only as much as they need. We may also share data when required by law or a government authority.

Storage and security

Your data is stored on secure servers with access restricted to authorised persons. No system is fully secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. After you close your account, we delete or anonymise it within 90 days, except records we must keep by law, such as payment and tax records.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent. Write to us at parmargeeta985@gmail.com.
