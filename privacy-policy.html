My Fleet

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

My Fleet helps authorized finance and fleet teams manage customers, vehicles, loan records, renewals, dues, documents and marketplace activity.

Information we collect

  • Account information such as name, email address, login ID and authentication information.
  • Contact information entered by an authorized administrator or customer.
  • Finance information including loan account numbers, lender details, loan amounts, EMI details, interest rates and repayment schedules.
  • Bank PDFs, identity or ownership documents, vehicle photos and other uploaded files.
  • Marketplace listings, chat messages, notes, attachments, task activity and audit history.

How we use information

  • Authenticate users and provide role-based access.
  • Manage fleet, customer, loan, EMI, renewal and collection workflows.
  • Read bank PDFs and extract finance fields when the user chooses that feature.
  • Provide marketplace listings, chat, notifications, reports and support.
  • Protect the service, prevent misuse and maintain audit records.

Third-party services and sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only as needed with authorized backend, database and service providers to operate the app. Bank PDFs are read locally in the browser using OCR. WhatsApp or other external services opened by the user handle information under their own privacy policies.

Security

Production data is transmitted through HTTPS. Access is protected with authenticated sessions and role-based permissions. Users should keep their credentials confidential.

Retention and deletion

We retain information while the account or business relationship is active and as needed for legitimate operational, legal or accounting purposes. To request deletion or correction, email privacy@aakashfinance.com with the organization name and account identifier.

Children

My Fleet is a business fleet-management service and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

Updates will be published on this page with a new effective date.
